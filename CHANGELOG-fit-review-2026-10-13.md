- **Backup tag:** `proposal-v5-pre-fit-review`.

## What the check found

| Area | Finding | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Subject and fit with Communication & Culture | The problem was framed mainly through HCI. It did not name the communication field that describes exactly what the system does. | Framed it as **AI-mediated communication**, using Hancock, Naaman & Levy's (2020) definition, quoted from the abstract. Human–machine communication (Guzman & Lewis 2020) was added as the contrasting vocabulary. Both were verified on Crossref and from their abstracts on 2026-10-13. |
| Novelty | The contribution was stated so cautiously that the new element was hard to see. | §1 now has an explicit statement. The closest studies mostly treat AI as a partner, performer or group resource that people create *with*. This project studies AI as a *medium* people create *through*: a translator between collaborators. §10 adds the contribution to communication studies, which is to extend AI-mediated communication from messages between individuals to collective creative production. All claims remain "possibilities, not predicted findings". |
| Fit with Dr. Caines | The proposal cited her work, but not her own current framing of AI. | §14 quotes Caines (2026, p. 193): AI work "as a kind of critical improvisation practice", with "a deep responsibility to how this material is utilized" and "a constant cycle of navigating power, adaptation, surprise, failure, and creation". The wording was checked against the CC BY full text. Her study centred on course design ("focusing mostly on the course/workshop design phase"). This project carries that framing to improvisers negotiating one another's contributions. |
| Creativity and research-creation | The creative output was described only as workshops and their documentation. | Improvisation scores for AI-mediated group work are now named as a creative output, credited to the groups that shaped them with their consent. No public performance or other commitment was added. |
| Quality | The research questions, methods, ethics and scale were sound and are unchanged. | Repetition was cut so that the additions fit under the ceiling. |
