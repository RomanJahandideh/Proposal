## Other changes required by the brief

- **Research questions:** rewritten around one central question and three subquestions. Each subquestion names its evidence. The text states that none assumes more provenance, control or AI is better, and that refusal and non-use are meaningful outcomes.
- **Related-work table (B only):** replaced the HCI-heavy table. It now has 7 rows mixing studies and artworks, with columns for AI role and opacity, participation and decision power, credit and data governance, and what the project adds.
- **Figure:** redesigned as a research-creation schematic with feedback loops and cross-cutting bands for documentation, consent and credit governance, and iteration. It appears in both B and C, and the evidence arrows were fixed so all four reach the dissertation box.
- **Ethics:** tiered, revisable consent; a non-AI route; no sensitive data sent to commercial services; disclosure of provider retention; the four senses of "ownership"; withdrawal limits after exhibition; power and accessibility; no demand that participants perform trauma; platform contingencies.
- **Contributions:** stated at five levels (conceptual, empirical, methodological, artistic, practical), without promising findings.
- **Plan of study (A):** four prose paragraphs; the table was removed.
- **Bibliography:** corrected Caines & Watson to pp. 253–277 and Fragile Devices to 2021. Removed citations that could not be verified (section G).
