The project belongs to Technology in Practice because it examines critically
how GenAI platforms, interfaces and institutional conditions configure
cultural practice, and to Media \& Culture because it studies cultural
production, representation, participation, authorship and labour. It
answers the program's aim of ``promoting the application of critical theory
and research'' \citep{york_overview} and its PhD objectives, which name
``creative exhibitions'' among the routes to independent research
\citep{york_phdpage}, while keeping the written dissertation as the site of
sustained argument. Dr.\ Caines's work on critical improvisation, socially
responsive creative technologies and GenAI is its closest ground; her GenAI
study focused mostly on course and workshop design
\citep[176]{caines2026steaming}; this project asks how culturally
situated contributions and authorship are negotiated in sustained collective
art-making and then made public. How it might relate to her SSHRC Insight
project, \emph{Interfacing, Playtesting, Resetting} \citep{yfile2026sshrc},
or to her other case studies would be designed with her later; nothing here
assumes access, participants, funding or a venue.
