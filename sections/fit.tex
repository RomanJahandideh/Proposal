The project is situated primarily within Technology in Practice because it
critically examines how GenAI platforms, interfaces and institutions
configure cultural practice. It is situated secondarily within Media \&
Culture because it studies cultural production, representation,
participation, authorship and labour. Its object is mediation, participation and
power rather than a model's technical performance: it treats platforms as
cultural infrastructures, draws on critical theory, and uses creative
practice as a method of inquiry whose installation is implicated in the
dissertation's argument \citep{comcult_rc}. Dr.\ Caines's work on critical improvisation and
GenAI is its closest scholarly ground; her GenAI study focused mostly on course and workshop
design \citep[176]{caines2026steaming}, whereas this project follows how
collaborators negotiate one another's contributions in sustained art-making
and exhibition.
