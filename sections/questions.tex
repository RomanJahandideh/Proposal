\textbf{Central question.} How does collaborative artistic practice with
generative AI redistribute participation, cultural voice and authorship, and
how can research-creation make those redistributions visible and
contestable?

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{SQ\arabic*.}, leftmargin=*]
  \item \emph{Translation.} How do different GenAI tools, interfaces, model
        defaults and facilitation arrangements translate, amplify,
        standardize, misread or pass over participants' culturally situated
        contributions? (Studio records; workshop episodes; prompts and
        outputs.)
  \item \emph{Negotiation.} How do participants negotiate recognition,
        refusal, credit, ownership and responsibility among individual
        contributors, the group, me as artist-facilitator, the models and the
        platforms behind them? (Episodes; group-authored rules; focus
        groups.)
  \item \emph{Forms and protocols.} What collaborative protocols and
        artistic forms emerge through the workshops and the public artwork,
        and what do they suggest about more accountable and culturally plural
        GenAI-mediated cultural production, including where not using GenAI
        is the better choice? (Scores; artwork iterations; focus groups.)
\end{enumerate}

None of these assumes that more provenance, more individual control or more
AI improves collaboration. Refusal, ambiguity, silence, failure and non-use
are treated as meaningful outcomes.
