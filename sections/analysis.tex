Analysis combines four strands: reflexive thematic analysis of focus groups
and follow-ups \citep{braun2019}; multimodal analysis of selected workshop
episodes, adapting the video microethnography \citet{mcarthur2015} used for
interface interaction; analysis of artifacts, prompts, outputs, revisions,
refusals and scores, charting each version of the artwork against the
contributors who shaped it, after the version timelines of \citet[10]{hodson2019}; and critical
reading of how platform conditions and my own choices shaped what was
possible. An episode runs from a contribution through its transformation and
the group's response to the next creative action. Selection will include
routine uptake, non-response, refusal, conflict, misrecognition and
unexpected collective invention, and each episode is read against its
institutional and cultural conditions. Focus-group themes and episodes are
interpreted together; neither counts as the true account. Triangulation,
analytic memos, a decision trail and discrepant cases support rigour. The design
cannot isolate ``the effect of AI'' from facilitation, platform, group
history and institution, and the tools will change during the study, so
claims will be situated and analytically transferable, not causal.
