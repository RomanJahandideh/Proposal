Analysis is aligned with the subquestions. For SQ1, I will analyze selected
workshop episodes multimodally, adapting the video microethnography
\citet{mcarthur2015} used for interface interaction, and read interfaces,
terms of service and model documentation as cultural documents. For SQ2,
reflexive thematic analysis of the focus groups \citep{braun2019} is read
alongside episodes and group-authored rules. For SQ3, version analysis charts
each state of the artwork against the contributors who shaped it, after the
version timelines of \citet[10]{hodson2019}, together with the studio journal
and failure archive. Episode selection includes routine uptake, refusal,
conflict, misrecognition and unexpected invention. These materials are put in dialogue rather than
triangulated into one objective account, with attention to discrepant cases,
reflexive memos and a transparent decision trail. The design cannot isolate
``the effect of AI'' from facilitation, platform, group history and
institution, and tools will change during the study, so claims will be
situated and analytically transferable, not causal.
