I am an artistic practitioner and researcher working through
research-creation. I am currently completing an MA in Interactive Arts and
Technology after training in architecture and landscape architecture, and I
create interactive 3D environments and multiplayer systems that examine how
technical mediation shapes relations among participants. This project asks
how GenAI systems, including models, interfaces and the platforms through
which they are accessed, reorganize interpretive authority and collective
authorship in collaborative artistic practice. Its subquestions ask how
tools, defaults and facilitation privilege, flatten or misrecognize
participants' culturally situated contributions; how collaborators negotiate
uptake, refusal, credit and responsibility; and how artistic methods and a
public artwork can make these hidden distributions of authority open to
debate and revision. The stakes are cultural: platforms increasingly
shape whose meanings become legible and who answers for shared work. The study begins with my own studio inquiry, then
consultation with paid practitioners, three workshops for each of three
cohorts of six to eight adult artists and community-engaged practitioners
with participant observation, semi-structured focus groups, and a co-created
room-scale installation proposed for exhibition at York. It aims to
contribute a Communication \& Culture account of how GenAI redistributes
interpretive authority and a research-creation method for tracing
participant-defined contributions, within the program's Technology in
Practice and Media \& Culture fields.
