Consent will be separate, ongoing and revisable \citep{strengers2021} for
workshop participation, recording, processing by third-party AI services,
use of quotations and artifacts, public exhibition, identifiable credit and
later dissemination. Every activity has a manual, non-AI route, and anyone
may refuse a tool or output without losing their place. No sensitive
personal data will go to commercial services; I will prefer local or
institutionally approved tools, disclose that providers may retain prompts
and outputs under their current terms, and review tool choices at ethics
approval, as Caines did when weighing privacy tiers against cost
\citep[187--188]{caines2026steaming}. Workshops will distinguish technical
provenance, legal ownership, public credit and felt authorship, and discuss
copyright and training-data uncertainty openly. Groups will agree in advance
on the limits of withdrawal once shared material has been exhibited. Credit
will be negotiated; I will seek funding for honoraria but do not assume it.
I will attend to power between myself and participants and between more and
less technically experienced participants, and support visual, sonic,
physical, linguistic and technical participation, with alternatives to
sound-only or vision-only interaction and help at every level of AI literacy. When models misread culturally specific expression,
participants will not be asked to perform trauma or to educate me.
Participation alone does not make the
project equitable \citep{birhane2022}.
