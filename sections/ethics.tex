Consent will be tiered and revisable \citep{strengers2021}, given separately
for recording, model input, research analysis, exhibition, documentation and
reuse. Anyone may refuse GenAI or any output and remain an equal creative
participant, because every activity has a manual route. No identifiable
voice, image or text, and no culturally sensitive material, will be sent to
a model without specific consent. Commercial services may retain or reuse
prompts and outputs under their terms; participants will be told this, and I
will prefer local or institutionally approved tools and review choices at
ethics approval. Groups will agree in advance how
withdrawal works once material has been remixed: identifiable contributions
are removed where feasible, but exhibited composite work may not be fully
separable. Legal ownership, platform terms, public credit, felt authorship
and research authorship are treated as distinct. Data will be minimized,
stored securely with restricted access, and retained and deleted on a stated
schedule. Participants and consultants will receive honoraria and access
costs; if funding falls short, I will reduce the sample or the number of
sessions rather than rely on unpaid artistic labour. I will support visual, sonic, physical, linguistic and technical
participation, with alternatives to sound-only or vision-only interaction and
help at every level of AI literacy, and participants will not be asked to
perform trauma or educate me about misrecognition. Participation alone does
not make the process equitable \citep{birhane2022}.
