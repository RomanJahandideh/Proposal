Three problems of access are foreseeable. \emph{Participants:} recruitment
depends on open calls; if a cohort cannot finish, its completed sessions
remain usable as a smaller case. \emph{Venue:} the gallery is a proposal; if
it is unavailable, the work will be shown in another campus space or as a
documented installation. \emph{Tools:} platforms may close, change terms or
prices, or update models, so Phase 1 keeps substitutes, including a local
model, and records versions.

\textbf{Expandable case-study architecture.} The core doctoral study is
self-contained. After the initial studio inquiry, the project is designed to
accommodate one bounded case-study strand developed with Dr.\ Caines. Her
SSHRC project \emph{Interfacing, Playtesting, Resetting}
\citep{yfile2026sshrc} is a possible point of connection, because both
inquiries concern improvisation, playtesting and how creative technologies
organize participation. Such a strand could take one of three forms: a
comparative analysis of authorized project materials; an embedded creative
workshop or playtesting context; or a public research-creation or exhibition
context. It would extend, not replace, the core research questions, and would
require separate agreement on roles, access, community or partner
governance, consent, data, ethics, resources, credit and outputs. The exact
relationship, if any, would be determined with Dr.\ Caines and relevant
partners. The doctoral study does not depend on that connection.
