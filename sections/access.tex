If a cohort cannot finish, its
completed sessions remain usable as a smaller case; if the gallery is
unavailable, the work will be shown elsewhere or as a documented

\textbf{Expandable case-study architecture.} After the initial studio
inquiry, one bounded case-study strand may be co-developed with Dr.\ Caines
around the most appropriate case from her current research, subject to her
guidance and any partner approvals. Her work on improvisation, playtesting
and socially responsive creative technologies offers several possible
connections, but no particular project relationship is assumed at this
stage; her SSHRC project \emph{Interfacing, Playtesting, Resetting}
\citep{yfile2026sshrc}, for example, may offer a possible context, subject to
Dr.\ Caines's guidance and partner approval. Any integration would extend,
rather than determine, the core research questions and would require
separate agreement concerning roles, access, community or partner
governance, consent, data, ethics, resources, credit and outputs. The
doctoral study remains complete without any one external case study.
